# Repository Working Notes

Use `AGENTS.md` as the engineering contract. The central design rule is that admitted edge contracts own domain invariants while Pingora remains a delivery adapter. `edge_routing` and `http_policy` may contain executable consumer-derived characterization only when the responsibility is genuinely shared edge behavior; they do not own product authorization, business routing, domain response semantics, Wardnet/EgressWeave verdicts, or Keyverse identity. `downstream_tls` owns only transport-neutral listener TLS/ALPN values; certificate lifecycle and key custody stay outside the gateway, while `tls_delivery` is the Pingora adapter that materializes already-provisioned certificate/key references.

Before a material change, compare the proposed module/path/API/config name with `PRD.md`, `TRD.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `CONTEXT_MAP.md`, and `UBIQUITOUS_LANGUAGE.md`. After the change, update tests and the gap baseline through its active owner lane. Do not claim release, migration, performance, SBOM/provenance, graceful-drain verification, image digest evidence, TLS/H2 parity, or HTTP/3 support unless the exact protected revision proves it.

The generic production root intentionally supports one upstream per process. Version 1 remains cleartext; the opt-in version 2 increment adds required downstream TLS material references and the explicit `h2_http1` ALPN policy without widening routing or product semantics. The stacked pg-erd migration candidate remains a separate composition root: its fixed characterized route table and response-security policy can be activated only by `cwl-pingora-pg-erd-migration` from bounded `PgErdMigrationConfig` with explicit `backend` and `frontend` transport authority. Pg-erd versions 1 and 2 remain cleartext; version 3 retains version-2 response-lifetime semantics and adds the same downstream TLS/H2 boundary. These candidates are not protected releases or consumer cutovers.

Adding arbitrary route-table or response-policy configuration, load balancing, WebSocket behavior, h2c, HTTP/3/QUIC, static serving, dynamic reload, or Kubernetes Gateway API remains a separate versioned product increment. Complete H2-downstream to H1-upstream parity remains gated by release-qualified supplier disposition of the Cookie coalescing and zero-length body-framing roots tracked in issue #51; do not vendor mutable supplier PRs to make that claim.
